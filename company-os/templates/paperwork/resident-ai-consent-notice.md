# Resident AI Notice & Consent Form — 模板

> **内部模板，非法律意见。⚠️ 需律师确认措辞后才能对外使用。** 复制后填写，不改本模板；不写真实居民信息。

| 项目 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| 用途 | 机构向居民（或其 supporter / 支持决策人 / 法定代理人）说明 CareKind AI 如何处理其护理记录，并记录同意或拒绝 |
| 何时使用 | 机构上线前（现有居民）；新居民入住时；同意撤回或支持决策安排变更时 |
| 谁填写 | 机构取得同意并保管原件；管理员在 CareKind 后台录入同意状态（PRD D8）。CareKind 只提供模板，不代取同意 |
| 保存年限 | 按客户政策【需律师确认】；建议随居民记录保存 |
| 法规依据 | Privacy Act APP 3（敏感信息收集同意）、APP 5（告知）；NSW HRIP Act 2002（HPP）；Ahpra AI 指南（首步明确同意）；ACSQHC Ambient scribe scenario；Aged Care Act 2024 支持决策框架；见 [recording-consent.md](../../03-legal-compliance/recording-consent.md)、[privacy-program.md](../../03-legal-compliance/privacy-program.md)、[federal-law-summary §2、§5](../../03-legal-compliance/research/federal-law-summary.md) |
| 版本 | 需提供易读版及常见社区语言译本（翻译件同样需审定） |

## 中文说明（给机构工作人员，不发给居民）

- 下方英文告知用通俗英文（Plain English）写成，建议在居民理解的语言和方式下逐段讲解，必要时使用口译。
- 有决策能力的居民本人签署；认知障碍居民按其 supporter / 法定代理人安排（各州监护法不同，【需律师确认】）。
- 拒绝或撤回不影响居民获得的照护；未同意的居民，CareKind 不处理与其相关的语句，护理人员手动记录。
- MVP 为按键口述（模式 A）：护理人员通常在离开房间后对设备口述，不录居民对话。

## Notice to residents（Plain English）

**What is CareKind?** Our care staff may use an app called CareKind to help write your care notes. After caring for you, a staff member speaks into the app. The app turns their words into a draft note.

**What it does not do.** It does not record your conversations. It does not diagnose, give medical or medication advice, or make decisions about your care.

**A person always checks.** Every draft is checked, corrected if needed, and signed by a staff member before it becomes part of your record. The staff member is responsible for the note.

**Your information stays in Australia.** Voice recordings and notes are stored and processed in Australia. Voice recordings are deleted soon after the note is signed.

**Not used to train AI.** Your information is not used to train AI models.

**Your choice.** You can say no. Saying no will not change your care. Staff will write your notes by hand instead.

**Changing your mind.** You can withdraw at any time by telling 【机构联系人职位】 or calling 【机构电话】. It takes effect straight away.

**Questions or complaints.** Contact 【机构隐私联系人】. You can also contact the Aged Care Quality and Safety Commission or the OAIC.【联系方式需律师确认】
